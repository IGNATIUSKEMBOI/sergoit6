Our Story

A Legacy of Excellence

Sergoit Primary School has served the communities of Uasin Gishu County with distinction for over three decades. From humble beginnings, we have grown into one of the most respected learning institutions in the region.

Our newly constructed two-storey classroom block and modern facilities reflect the commitment of our school community, the county government, and generous supporters who believe in quality education for every child.

Today, Sergoit Primary School is home to over 1,200 learners and 45 dedicated teachers, delivering the Competency Based Curriculum with passion and excellence.
