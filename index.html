Sergoit Primary School stands as a beacon of academic excellence in Uasin Gishu County. Our newly completed modern facilities, dedicated faculty, and vibrant student community make us one of the premier learning institutions in the region.

We deliver the Competency Based Curriculum (CBC) with passion and precision, ensuring every learner is holistically developed — academically, socially, and morally.
